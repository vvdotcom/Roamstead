5. A live ADK workflow retrieves vector memory, analyzes three properties, runs two Gemma critics in parallel, joins them, verifies evidence, applies bounded correction routing, composes a Decision Brief, and persists it.
6. The city selector proves two additional, relevant Google model integrations: cached Veo orientation video and cached Gemini TTS narration, both persisted once and excluded from property evidence.
6. The completed brief visibly proves successful use of `gemini-embedding-001`, `gemma-4-26b-a4b-it`, and `gemma-4-31b-it` with distinct product outputs.
7. An approval-gated Decision Watch performs selected evidence checks and writes immutable revisions.
8. Reload restores the profile, brief, audit results, watch, and trace.
